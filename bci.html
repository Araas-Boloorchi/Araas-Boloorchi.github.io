Brain-Controlled Wheelchair

Brain-Computer Interface • 2016

A brain-computer interface project from 2016: I used sensorimotor signals, recorded with an EEG headset, to move a wheelchair.

How it worked

  • A 3D-printed EEG headset recorded brain activity over the sensorimotor area
  • The sensorimotor signals were classified into movement commands
  • Those commands drove the wheelchair

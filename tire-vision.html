Tire Deformation Tracking

Automated Tire, Inc. · Machine Vision & ML · 2026

The platform: Automated Tire's SmartBay, which changes tires without taking the wheel off the vehicle — see it in The Robot Report's introduction video.

During a robotic tire change the tire is compressed and deforms, and the process needs to watch for small movements while that happens. A compressed rubber tire doesn't hold a fixed shape, so tracking its geometry with deterministic measurements alone is very difficult.

What I did

  • Evaluated and selected new machine vision hardware for the project
  • Developed a novel vision model that monitors subtle movements during the tire change process
  • Took it from idea to a working ML prototype in a short period, with millimetre-scale sensitivity

Recommendation
